`integerToCurrency` and `integerToCurrencyWithDecimal` now take `decimalPlaces` as the second argument, with the formatter as an optional third. Both helpers now respect the active currency precision and `hideFraction` setting when no formatter is supplied. The two existing callers passing a formatter (`useFormat`, `actions.transferAvailable`) have been updated.
