Tighten the `amountToInteger`, `integerToAmount`, `appendDecimals`, `currencyToInteger`, `toRelaxedNumber`, `integerToCurrency`, and `integerToCurrencyWithDecimal` helpers to require an explicit `decimalPlaces` argument. The temporary two-decimal default added during the rollout is removed; every consumer now passes the active currency's precision.
