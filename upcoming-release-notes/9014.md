`CloseAccountModal`, `CreateLocalAccountModal`, `EditFieldModal`, `EnvelopeBudgetMenuModal`, `TrackingBudgetMenuModal`, and `ImportTransactionsModal` now thread the active currency's decimal places through their amount conversions and display formatting. `EditFieldModal` also rejects amounts whose fractional digits exceed the currency precision instead of silently truncating.
